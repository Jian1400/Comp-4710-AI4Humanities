How to read it

Each bar shows the total unique To/From correspondence for that year among the people currently visible. A dot marks a person who exchanged correspondence that year; larger dots mean more letters. Click a year bar to drill into months. Click a dot or person name to inspect the underlying letters. Dates with a known year but no reliable month remain in UNK rather than being guessed.
